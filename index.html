About

Currently a research collaborator at ASAM e.V. and a recent M.Sc. Mechatronics graduate (Hochschule Ravensburg-Weingarten), I specialize in perception for autonomous systems — training segmentation and detection models in PyTorch, fusing camera, radar and LiDAR data, and getting the result running in real time with ONNX and TensorRT. My master's thesis at IAV GmbH took automotive camera soiling detection from an open research question to a deployed model. I'm looking for a full-time role in drone perception, SLAM, or autonomous systems, and I believe my mechanical background, maker mentality, and enthusiasm for the field will be an asset to any team I join.
