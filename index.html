View Projects

Knowledge-Grounded Industrial VLM

A local, knowledge-grounded vision-language system for industrial anomaly detection. Retrieval-augmented reasoning grounds a quantized VLM (Qwen2.5-VL) with domain knowledge and visually similar defect examples, running on just 8GB of VRAM. Built with PyTorch, Transformers, FAISS, and embedding-based retrieval.

Physical AI Pick-and-Place

A MuJoCo simulation of a Franka arm that picks and places objects using ArUco-marker point prompts segmented by FLIP (Fovea-Like Input Patching) — no shape-specific training required. Classifies square, circle, and triangle objects from the segmented mask's geometry and sorts them into matching bins.

ROS 2 Drone Interceptor

A ROS 2 package for autonomous drone-vs-drone pursuit. An onboard vision node tracks a randomly evading target in Gazebo while a Double DQN with Prioritized Experience Replay selects intercept maneuvers via ArduPilot SITL and MAVROS2. Ships as a Dockerized six-service stack for one-command reproduction, with TensorBoard logs and a trained checkpoint.

Cell Segmentation with MobileViT

A complete AI workflow for cervical cytology, integrating Cellpose for cell segmentation, MobileViT for classification on the SIPaKMeD dataset, and FastAPI for automated clinical PDF report generation.

ArUco-Guided FLIP Segmentation

An independent exploration of ArUco-marker-guided point-prompt segmentation using FLIP, inspired by a Grounding Engine concept — benchmarked against a public YOLOv8 baseline on novel bottle shapes.

Outdoor Gaussian Splatting (TEDGS)

Real-world outdoor scene reconstruction using COLMAP and 3D Gaussian Splatting, built from a custom smartphone-captured dataset.

MedVisionAI

Classifies brain MRI scans as healthy or tumorous, comparing a CNN built from scratch against MobileNet-based transfer learning. Transfer learning reaches ~97% test accuracy (~100% validation) versus ~92% for the from-scratch CNN.

Platooning Autonomous Following Robot

A cost-effective, autonomous robot that follows a lead vehicle in a platoon formation — using computer vision and ArUco markers to track the leader and maintain a safe distance without human intervention. A companion Leader Robot in the same project uses LiDAR and camera-based lane detection to guide the platoon.

Multi-Sensor Fusion for Shape Classification

Fuses images from three simulated sensors — average, max, and min fusion — and classifies shapes (circle, square, triangle, pentagon) with a modified LeNet-5 CNN in PyTorch. A majority-voting ensemble across the fused streams reaches 86.5% accuracy on a 5,000+ image synthetic dataset.

Vehicle Distance Estimation via Sensor Fusion

Fuses LiDAR, radar, and camera data from the Astyx HiRes2019 dataset to estimate vehicle distance more accurately than any single sensor. YOLOv5 detects vehicles in-frame, LiDAR and radar points are projected onto the image, and a Kalman filter fuses the two ranging modalities into a refined distance estimate.

Robot Car Racing Competition

A Raspberry Pi 4 robot car built across four embedded-systems tasks: real-time line following, LiDAR-based obstacle stopping 20cm before collision, color-cube detection and approach, and ArUco-marker cube detection — all running on OpenCV, NumPy, and RPi.GPIO.

Object Detection and Depth Estimation

YOLOv8 object detection on the KITTI dataset paired with camera-calibration-based depth estimation, comparing predicted distances against ground truth. Reaches 85% detection accuracy within a 5-meter range, with scatter-plot analysis quantifying depth error across scenes.

Augmented Reality with ArUco Markers

Overlays poster images onto real-world scenes by detecting ArUco markers with OpenCV, computing a homography-based transformation, and blending the poster into the marker's perspective. Successfully overlaid posters on 10 of 11 test images.

Evaluation of a Bird's-Eye-View Object Detector

Evaluates a YOLO-based detector on Bird's-Eye-View images from the KITTI dataset, computing IoU, precision, and recall across multiple scenes to quantify detection performance and surface failure cases in complex scenes.

Influence of Fog on LiDAR and Radar Sensors

Compares Blickfeld Cube and Velodyne Puck LiDAR against MMWAVCAS-RF-EVM radar under clear vs. foggy conditions, processing 500+ CSV recordings per sensor per scenario and analyzing RMS-value distributions to quantify how fog degrades each sensor's measurements.
